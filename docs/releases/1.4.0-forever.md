# 1.4.0-forever

Add an optional XP speedometer with a configurable scale and streamline tracking
updates, panel interaction and character display preferences.

Changes are compared with [v1.3.1](https://github.com/eavidaze/wow-addon-per-hour/releases/tag/v1.3.1),
the previous published Forever release. The release title and tag are `v1.4.0`.

## Added

- Enable **Show speedometer** under Experience in **Settings** to display an XP
  gauge above the existing numeric rate. Choose one of ten scale levels; changing
  between hourly and per-minute units keeps the selected level. The gauge is
  disabled by default and its settings are saved per character. Honor and
  Reputation retain their numeric displays.

## Changed

- Use one **Per hour** or **Per minute** preference for all three trackers, saved
  per character instead of separate account-wide choices. Both rates remain
  available in session tooltips.
- Show overload with a warning color and short native pointer tremors separated
  by pauses. The pointer and its shadow move together; the numeric readout keeps
  the actual rate above the scale maximum.
- Refresh active tracking through one shared timer, with XP and reputation
  events capturing gains between periodic samples. Hidden panels continue
  tracking and synchronize their rate display when shown. Unchanged controls and
  readouts avoid repeated interface updates.
- Keep background dragging responsive and save changed panel and minimap
  positions when dragging ends, when hidden during dragging, or on logout.
- Show tracker close buttons instantly on hover, including over child controls,
  without fading. Restore indicators also appear without fading: success hides
  after five seconds; unresolved errors remain until **Resume** or **Reset**,
  then hide after half a second.
- Center compact headers and footers around their content and align nested
  settings controls, keeping the XP gauge toggle beside its scale control.

## Compatibility and limitations

- Targets WoW Forever 1.60.1, Interface `16001`.
- Preserves existing per-character layouts and saved sessions. A character
  without a saved display unit imports the previous account choices only when
  all three valid choices agree; mixed, missing or invalid choices default to
  hourly. Previous account preferences remain intact for other characters.
- The default gauge level is 5, representing 800 XP/min or approximately 50k XP/h.
  Hourly and per-minute scale maxima are rounded and are not exact conversions.
- Session continuity retains its 30-second played-time tolerance and depends on
  normal logout or UI reload for disk persistence. Missing progress cannot be
  recovered.

- Automated: 1,727 simulation checks passed with Lua 5.1 through Python's
  `lupa.lua51` runner, including character settings, XP events and level-ups,
  reputation, Honor Points, pause/resume, restore timers, mouse routing, dragging
  and native overload state changes. Frame simulations cover 60 and 144 FPS;
  callback and interface-call counts do not measure actual WoW CPU time or FPS.
- Local package: `per-hour-1.4.0-forever.zip` checked for the `per-hour/` root,
  matching version and Interface, 33 files matching the source, 26 TOC/XML load
  references with exact filename casing and excluded development files.
- Complete in-game validation remains pending. Confirm gauge rendering, pointer
  and shadow tremor, hover across controls, dragging, all settings fields and
  reload/login behavior on two characters. See [panel validation](../panels.md),
  [session continuity](../session-continuity.md#validation) and
  [performance validation](../performance.md).
