# 1.2.0-forever

This release preserves tracker sessions across UI reloads and logins, checks
their continuity against played time, and refreshes the tracker panels.

Changes are compared with [v1.1.1](https://github.com/eavidaze/wow-addon-per-hour/releases/tag/v1.1.1),
the previous published Forever release, whose TOC version was `1.1.1-forever`.
The GitHub release title and tag for this version are `v1.2.0`.

## Added

- Per-character session persistence for XP, honor and reputation, including
  totals, active duration, manual pause state and the watched faction.
- A shared played-time continuity check on reload or login. Matching sessions
  resume their previous state; unverified sessions preserve their saved totals
  and stay paused until Resume accepts them or Reset clears them.

## Changed

- Tracker panels use translucent native borders, centered titles and gold
  controls. Drag anywhere on the panel background to move it.
- Close buttons fade in while hovering over the panel and fade out when leaving.
- A top-left restore indicator and tooltip explain pending, successful or failed
  continuity checks. Successful checks disappear after 5 seconds; resolved errors
  disappear after a 0.5-second delay. Fade animations take 0.08 seconds.
- Active duration continues from saved session time, excluding time offline,
  initialization time and manually paused time.

## Fixed

- Ignore invalid or temporarily reduced honor readings so their recovery does
  not inflate tracked gains.
- Align the minimap watch icon with the visible border.

## Compatibility and limitations

- Targets WoW Forever 1.60.1, Interface `16001`.
- Continuity uses one played-time reference per character with a 30-second
  tolerance. A missing or failed query, or a 10-second timeout, requires review.
  A successful time check also waits for readable tracker data.
- Saving to disk depends on a normal logout or UI reload. The check cannot
  recover lost progress, and interruptions within the tolerance may go unnoticed.
- Older version 1 session snapshots retain their saved results but need Resume
  or Reset once because they have no played-time reference. New saves use version 2.
- Reputation sessions reset when the watched faction changes.

## Validation

- Automated: 505 simulation checks passed using Lua 5.1 through Python's
  `lupa.lua51` runner, including session saves and restores, pause states,
  played-time mismatches and timeouts, loading screens, panel dragging and fades.
- Local package: `per-hour-1.2.0-forever.zip` generated and checked for the
  `per-hour/` root, matching version and Interface, 24 files matching the source,
  19 TOC/XML load references with exact filename casing, and excluded development files.
- In-game validation remains pending. Follow [Session continuity](../session-continuity.md#validation)
  to check reloads, character selection, normal exits, interrupted saves and
  Resume/Reset. Also check panel dragging, native artwork and hover animations.
