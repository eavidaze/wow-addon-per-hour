# 1.6.0-forever

Track estimated net gold alongside XP, Honor and Reputation, and inspect the
income and expenses recorded during your farming session.

Changes are compared with [v1.5.0](https://github.com/eavidaze/wow-addon-per-hour/releases/tag/v1.5.0),
the previous published Forever release. The release title and tag are `v1.6.0`.

## Added

- Enable **Gold** from the minimap menu or Settings to track estimated net gold
  per hour or minute. Include looted money, quest money and received item values;
  subtract consumables used, valuable items delivered to completed quests,
  personal repairs and paid flights. Pause, Resume, Reset, hidden tracking,
  per-character session persistence and Announce work with Gold as well.
- Left-click the Gold tracker or choose **Gold → Options → Details** to open its
  scrolling event journal. Review income, expenses and estimated net profit;
  inspect event times, item quantities and native item/quest tooltips.
- Value eligible items with optional **TSM → Auctionator → vendor** pricing,
  using TSM's DBMarket or Auctionator's last scan with vendor as the minimum.
  Neither integration is required. The item tooltip in Details identifies the
  recorded price source and value, including portions valued at different prices.
- Freeze item prices when recorded. Consumables and quest deliveries use the
  oldest available recorded acquisition values first; unmatched stock receives
  a quote when used. Selling or transferring items removes their units from
  that allocation without adding income or a speculative expense.

## Changed

- Format Gold totals and announcements in gold, silver and copper, with decimal
  gold rates in the tracker. Details uses local event clock times, quantity
  suffixes, entry/exit arrows and distinct outgoing values.
- Keep valid per-character rate preferences. Characters without a valid saved
  hourly/per-minute preference now default to hourly instead of importing older
  account-level module preferences. If only the old account preference existed,
  select per-minute again in Settings; new modules no longer affect that choice.

## Fixed

- Recover pending item prices from the native cache after session verification,
  world entry and pause/resume, including replies received while restoration was
  pending. Accepted entries can settle while paused without counting new gains.
- Preserve already confirmed quest deliveries across pause/resume and loading
  screens within their original confirmation window, avoiding lost or duplicate
  item expenses when bag updates arrive later.
- Prevent sold, mailed, traded, banked or otherwise removed units from supplying
  stale acquisition prices to later replacements. Stock availability survives
  reload, including observed losses whose runtime confirmation was interrupted.

## Compatibility and limitations

- Targets WoW Forever 1.60.1, Interface `16001`.
- Gold describes estimated farming value, not cash profit or guaranteed Auction
  House proceeds. Item values count on receipt; selling them adds nothing again.
  Mail, trade, bank transfers, purchases, buybacks, crafting and guild-funded
  repairs remain outside income/expense accounting. Market prices are gross,
  without Auction House commission or deposits.
- Native item data may remain pending until loaded. Known zero-value items are
  omitted. Existing saved prices retain their policy; no previous transactions
  or settings are migrated or backfilled.
- Consumption and quest deliveries require matching native actions and readable
  bag-count decreases. Short confirmation windows, ambiguous events, gains that
  mask losses and unobserved offline movements can leave actions unrecognized.
  Unrecorded runtime action correlations do not survive reload.
- Item/quest tooltips, external addon APIs, event order and protected/secret
  values still require actual Forever validation. See [Gold tracking](https://github.com/eavidaze/wow-addon-per-hour/blob/v1.6.0/docs/gold-tracking.md)
  for detailed accounting and detection boundaries.

## Validation

- Automated: 2,882 simulation checks passed with Lua 5.1 through Python's
  `lupa.lua51` runner, covering Gold accounting, provider fallbacks, recorded
  prices, mixed acquisition lots, pending data, quest delivery, stock retirement,
  pause/resume, reload and Details tooltips, alongside existing tracker checks.
- Local package: `per-hour-1.6.0-forever.zip` checked for its `per-hour/` root,
  matching TOC version and Interface, exact-case TOC/XML load references,
  source-file contents and excluded development files.
- In-game validation remains pending. Verify real looting, quest turn-ins,
  consumables, personal/guild repairs, flights, transfer exclusions, replacement
  stock, TSM/Auctionator APIs, Details rendering and session continuity in
  WoW Forever. Simulation results do not establish gameplay compatibility.
