# 1.5.0-forever

Estimate the active time remaining until your next XP level or reputation
standing, using your current progress and the session's average earning rate.

Changes are compared with [v1.4.0](https://github.com/eavidaze/wow-addon-per-hour/releases/tag/v1.4.0),
the previous published Forever release. The release title and tag are `v1.5.0`.

## Added

- See the next level or standard reputation standing and its estimated time in
  the tracker tooltip, for example `Level 43 in 2h 13m` or
  `Honored in 1h 23m`. Predictions use the unrounded session mean and round
  remaining time up to whole minutes, with a minimum of one minute.
- Enable **Show estimates** in **Settings → General** to display the same
  prediction in small centered text below XP and Reputation rates. The option
  is disabled by default and saved per character. It works alongside the XP
  speedometer and does not affect estimates in tooltips.

## Changed

- Place **Estimate** before **Session** and **Rates** in XP and Reputation
  tooltips, below the tracking status and faction context.
- Match estimate text to the tracking button's diamond: gray before starting,
  green while running and yellow while paused. Button actions, status labels
  and estimate colors now share one tracking-status definition and DS palette.
- Refresh panel predictions through the existing shared timer, avoiding repeated
  writes of unchanged text and colors. The panel reserves space for the optional
  estimate line and synchronizes hidden displays when shown.

## Compatibility and limitations

- Targets WoW Forever 1.60.1, Interface `16001`.
- Honor has no estimate in this version. Friendship, renown and paragon
  reputations are excluded from standard-standing predictions.
- Predictions wait for one active minute. After that, zero or negative earning
  rates, unavailable progress and pending session restoration show
  **No estimate available**. Pausing shows **Paused**; maximum level and Exalted
  show the corresponding completed milestone instead of a duration.
- Predictions describe time at the session's average pace, including idle time
  while tracking and excluding pauses. They do not guarantee a completion time.
  Level and standing changes advance the goal; changing factions keeps the
  existing reputation-session reset.
- Existing settings and saved sessions are preserved. The new visibility
  preference defaults to disabled when absent; no migration is introduced.

## Validation

- Automated: 1,865 simulation checks passed with Lua 5.1 through Python's
  `lupa.lua51` runner, including estimates, minute rounding, unavailable progress,
  pause/resume, level and standing changes, faction resets, shared status colors,
  character preferences and reload persistence. Existing tracking, continuity,
  mouse-routing and performance simulations also passed.
- Local package: `per-hour-1.5.0-forever.zip` checked for its `per-hour/` root,
  matching TOC version and Interface, exact-case TOC/XML load references,
  source-file contents and excluded development files.
- In-game validation remains pending. Confirm tooltip and panel text, status
  colors, maximum-level detection, faction thresholds, the estimate checkbox,
  gauge coexistence and reload/login behavior in WoW Forever. Simulation checks
  do not establish actual rendering, gameplay behavior or performance.
